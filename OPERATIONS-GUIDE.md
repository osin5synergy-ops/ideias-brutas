# Operations Guide - 2w3-0 Sandbox Supply1 Crews

## Quick Start

### For New Team Members
1. Read the main [playbook document](./2w3-0-sandbox-supply1-playbook1-crews.md)
2. Review the [crew configuration](./crews-config.yaml)
3. Join your crew's communication channels
4. Attend the next daily standup

### Daily Operations Checklist

#### Every Morning (9:00 AM)
- [ ] Attend daily standup (15 minutes)
- [ ] Review assigned tasks in project board
- [ ] Check for blockers or dependencies
- [ ] Update task status

#### During Work Hours
- [ ] Follow your crew's specific protocols
- [ ] Maintain communication in dedicated channels
- [ ] Document progress and learnings
- [ ] Collaborate across crew boundaries when needed

#### End of Day
- [ ] Update task progress
- [ ] Note any blockers for tomorrow
- [ ] Prepare standup updates
- [ ] Commit and push code changes (if applicable)

## Emergency Procedures

### System Down
1. Alert all crews via #sandbox-updates
2. Check monitoring dashboards
3. Activate incident response team
4. Document timeline and actions

### Quality Issues
1. Stop deployment pipeline
2. Alert Crew Gamma immediately
3. Root cause analysis
4. Fix and re-test before proceeding

### Resource Conflicts
1. Escalate to crew leads
2. Review resource allocation
3. Adjust priorities if needed
4. Document resolution for future reference

## A FAISCA Protection Protocol

### 10% Innovation Time Rules
- **Protected Time**: 4 hours per week minimum
- **No Interruptions**: Unless critical system emergency
- **Documentation Required**: All experiments must be logged
- **Freedom to Fail**: Failures are learning opportunities

### Innovation Time Activities
- Explore new technologies
- Experiment with creative solutions
- Research industry trends
- Prototype radical ideas
- Learn new skills

## FAQ

### Q: What happens if I miss a daily standup?
**A**: Update your crew in Slack with your status and any blockers. Try to sync with a crew member who attended.

### Q: How do I request time off?
**A**: Submit request through HR system and notify your crew lead at least 1 week in advance.

### Q: What if my A FAISCA time gets interrupted?
**A**: Document the interruption and discuss with your crew lead to reschedule the protected time.

### Q: How do I suggest improvements to the playbook?
**A**: Create an issue in the GitHub repository or discuss in the next retrospective.

### Q: Who do I contact for technical support?
**A**: Post in #tech-support channel or contact IT helpdesk at support@ideias-brutas.com
